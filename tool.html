Annual Cap Rate
Each policy year, your index participation is subject to a cap — the maximum credited return regardless of how strongly the market performs. The cap is reviewed and reset by Allianz at each annual policy anniversary.
Rates shown are Allianz's current indicative rates for your investment amount and term. You can adjust the cap in this tool to model different scenarios.

Annual Return Scenarios
Each option is a single-year return drawn from the historical distribution of Euro Stoxx 50® annual returns.

Average — the median year: half of all historical years produced a higher return, half a lower one.

Pessimistic — 9 in 10 historical years produced a better return. Shows how the buffer or floor absorbs losses in a bad year.

Optimistic — only 1 in 10 historical years produced a better return. Shows upside participation, subject to the annual cap.

Mixing outcomes across years creates a hypothetical illustration. The combined result does not represent a defined probability for the full term.
Market Crash Scenario
One year's simulated return is replaced with a historical return from a period when the Euro Stoxx 50® fell by more than 10%. This shows how the buffer or floor absorbs losses in a genuine downturn. The severity reflects a typical historical crash — not the most extreme on record. All other years remain unchanged.
Cap Rate Stress Test
The cap is only guaranteed for Year 1. From Year 2 onwards it resets annually — so the Lower and Higher lines stress-test what happens if future caps settle consistently below or above today's rate. All three lines start from the same point because Year 1 is fixed.
Crediting Strategies
Each strategy applies a different level of downside protection in exchange for a cap on upside participation. Higher protection means a lower cap rate.
1
Protection Buffer 10: 10% Buffer
If the index falls within -10% during the policy year, you register no losses and your capital is guaranteed at 100%. If the index falls beyond -10%, your loss is limited to the amount beyond the buffer. For example, if the index falls 7% you lose nothing; if it falls 14%, you lose only 4%. Upside is subject to the annual cap. Suits investors comfortable carrying some tail risk in exchange for the highest cap rate of the three strategies.
2
Guarantee 95: −5% Floor
An annual guarantee of 95% of the coverage value applies. The floor on index returns is never below -5%. You absorb small declines in full, but any loss beyond 5% is covered by Allianz. Positive annual returns are credited and locked in to your guaranteed minimum. Suited to investors seeking a balance between protection and cap rate.
3
Guarantee 100: 0% Floor
An annual guarantee of 100% of the coverage value applies. The floor on index returns is never below 0%. Whatever the index does, you cannot lose money: if the index falls, your credited return is 0% and your capital is preserved. Positive annual returns are credited and locked in to your guaranteed minimum. Suited to investors prioritising full capital protection.
Summary Risk Indicator (SRI)
The SRI is the standardised 1–7 risk score required under the EU PRIIPs Regulation. 1 is lowest risk; 7 is highest. The score reflects combined market and credit risk. A lower SRI indicates stronger downside protection; a higher SRI reflects greater market exposure in exchange for a higher cap.

Performance Scenarios
Each scenario is one simulated return path, ranked by percentile across the full simulation set. Together they show the range of outcomes the current product terms could produce — they are not forecasts.
P10
Pessimistic — 10th Percentile
An adverse outcome: 9 in 10 simulations produced a better result. Illustrates downside risk and the value of capital protection. This is not a worst-case scenario — the model is calibrated on average historical conditions and may understate the severity of an extreme market event.
P50
Average — 50th Percentile
The median outcome across all simulations: half produced a higher result, half a lower one. This is not a guaranteed or expected return.
P90
Optimistic — 90th Percentile
A favourable outcome: only 1 in 10 simulations produced a better result. Illustrates upside participation subject to the applicable annual cap.
