Annual Cap Rate
Each policy year, your index participation is subject to a cap, the maximum credited return regardless of how strongly the market performs. Today's rate is guaranteed for your first policy year; from Year 2 it is reviewed and reset by Allianz at each annual anniversary.
Rates shown are Allianz's current indicative rates for your investment amount and term. Turn on Cap Stress to see an illustration of how your returns could vary under a stressed cap, applying a ±50% adjustment to today's rate. Actual future caps will differ from this illustration.

Annual Return Scenarios
Each preset scenario applies a fixed sequence of modelled annual index returns to show how the selected strategy would respond under different market conditions. These are illustrations, not forecasts. Each year type is a fixed percentile of an annual return distribution, fitted to the mean and volatility of the Euro Stoxx 50®'s historical monthly returns, then applied in a fixed sequence across the term.
What each year type means
A typical year: the 50th percentile return — a return in the middle of the modelled range of outcomes.
A weaker year: the 25th percentile return — a below-average, though not extreme, decline for the index.
A stronger year: the 75th percentile return — an above-average return for the index.
A crash year: the 10th percentile return — a sharp, historically significant fall, beyond an ordinary weaker year.
1
Stable Growth
A stable, positive market, with every year at the 50th percentile return — a typical year.
2
Crash and Recovery
A crash in Year 1 (10th percentile return), followed by a strong rebound in Year 2 (75th percentile return), then a typical year (50th percentile return) for any remaining years.
3
Strong Growth
Continuous strong growth, with every year at the 75th percentile return — a stronger year.
4
Declining Market
A prolonged downturn, with every year at the 25th percentile return — a weaker year — representing a sustained run of losses.
5
Volatile Market
A volatile market, alternating between a weaker year (25th percentile return) and a stronger year (75th percentile return) across the term.
6
Custom
Switch the toggle above to Custom to choose from the same percentile-based year types as the presets for each year, or type in your own returns.
These scenarios are hypothetical and for illustrative purposes only. Cap rates and protection levels shown are indicative and reset at each annual policy anniversary.

Crediting Strategies
Each strategy applies a different level of downside protection in exchange for a cap on upside participation. Higher protection means a lower cap rate.
1
Protection Buffer 10: 10% Buffer
If the index falls within -10% during the policy year, you register no losses and your capital is guaranteed at 100%. If the index falls beyond -10%, your loss is limited to the amount beyond the buffer. For example, if the index falls 7% you lose nothing; if it falls 14%, you lose only 4%. Upside is subject to the annual cap. Suits investors comfortable carrying some tail risk in exchange for the highest cap rate of the three strategies.
2
Guarantee 95: −5% Floor
An annual guarantee of 95% of the coverage value applies. The floor on index returns is never below -5%. You absorb small declines in full, but any loss beyond 5% is covered by Allianz. Positive annual returns are credited and locked in to your guaranteed minimum. Suited to investors seeking a balance between protection and cap rate.
3
Guarantee 100: 0% Floor
An annual guarantee of 100% of the coverage value applies. The floor on index returns is never below 0%. Whatever the index does, you cannot lose money: if the index falls, your credited return is 0% and your capital is preserved. Positive annual returns are credited and locked in to your guaranteed minimum. Suited to investors prioritising full capital protection.
Summary Risk Indicator (SRI)
The SRI is the standardised 1–7 risk score required under the EU PRIIPs Regulation. 1 is lowest risk; 7 is highest. The score reflects combined market and credit risk. A lower SRI indicates stronger downside protection; a higher SRI reflects greater market exposure in exchange for a higher cap.
