About the product

Allianz Active4Life Evolution è un prodotto assicurativo di tipo misto, Unit e Index Linked. Le coperture Index Linked, di durata 3 o 5 anni, prevedono 4 strategie di investimento. Potrai investire in una o più delle strategie e modificare le scelte nel corso della durata del contratto. Tutte le garanzie sono fornite direttamente dalla compagnia Allianz Global Life dac. Questo strumento illustra le 3 strategie indicizzate.

How your premium is invested
High-quality fixed income (bonds)
Hedging instruments

Each year, Allianz earns a yield from its investment portfolio. That yield funds operating expenses, product benefits and guarantees, and the hedging budget that makes index-linked returns possible. Any remaining income forms Allianz's profit.

The vast majority of your premium is invested in a diversified portfolio of high-quality fixed income assets, designed to grow back to at least your original investment over the contract term. This portfolio funds the guarantees and fixed interest benefits of the product, which are provided directly by Allianz Global Life dac according to the protection level of your chosen strategy.

A small share is used as a hedging budget — used to purchase financial instruments (options) that make it possible to offer index-linked returns on top of your capital protection. Without this hedging budget, Allianz could not offer the indexed returns that the strategies provide.

Cap rates
Cap Rate Mechanics
What determines the cap rate

You've seen that each strategy carries an annual cap — the maximum index return it can credit in any policy year. That cap is not fixed for the life of the contract; it is reviewed and may change at each annual policy anniversary based on market conditions. If your cap is 13% and the market rises 25%, you receive 13%. If the market rises 10%, you receive the full 10%.

The factors that set your cap rate

Each year, Allianz sets the cap rate based on a combination of interconnected factors that determine how much is available to fund your index-linked returns.

01 — Investment yield
Allianz invests your premium largely in fixed income assets. The performance of these assets is the primary source of funding for the cap rate. A higher yield means a larger hedging budget is available.
02 — Hedging budget
A portion of that income becomes the hedging budget: the pool used to buy options that deliver index-linked returns. Market conditions affect the size of this budget every year.
03 — Hedging cost
The hedging budget is used to build a portfolio of options. When option costs rise, the same budget buys less, which can limit the cap rate available.

Why do cap rates change?

These factors combine to set the overall budget available each year. The factors below then determine how that budget translates into your specific cap rate:

1
Investment yield & bond income
Bond yields directly affect the income available to fund cap rates. When yields are higher, more income flows into the hedging budget and cap rates tend to be higher. When yields fall, the budget contracts and cap rates drop accordingly.
Higher yields → higher caps
2
Options costs & market volatility
Allianz uses the hedging budget to build a portfolio of options on the index. In volatile markets, those options become more expensive, leaving less of the budget available for Guarantee 95 and Guarantee 100, so their cap rates tend to fall. Protection Buffer 10 is different: it sells options as part of its structure, earning more income when volatility rises. Its cap rate may improve in high-volatility conditions.
Diverges by strategy — see table below
3
Your protection choice
Every strategy requires Allianz to spend part of the hedging budget on downside protection. The more protection the strategy offers, the more of that budget is consumed on the downside, leaving less available to fund the cap on the upside. Your choice of strategy will therefore directly influence the cap rate available to you.
More protection → lower cap
4
Contract term
Cap rates vary based on the selected term. A longer policyholder commitment allows Allianz to invest in longer-duration bonds, which can generate more income and therefore a larger hedging budget. This may translate into a higher cap rate, though the difference will depend on market conditions at the time.
Longer term → higher cap

Cap rates are the net result of several interacting market factors. Improvements in one can be offset by adverse movements in another, meaning a favourable shift does not always produce a higher cap rate.
