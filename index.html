Crediting Strategies
Each strategy applies a different level of downside protection in exchange for a cap on upside participation. Higher protection means a lower cap rate.
Opportunity: 10% Buffer
The first 10% of any index loss is absorbed by the buffer — you only start to lose once the index falls beyond that. For example, if the index falls 8% you lose nothing; if it falls 15% you lose only 5%. Upside participation is subject to the applicable cap rate. Suited to investors comfortable accepting some residual downside risk in exchange for a higher cap.
Balance: −5% Floor
Your maximum loss is capped at 5%, regardless of how far the index falls. If the index falls 30% you lose only 5%; if it falls 3% you lose 3%. Provides a degree of downside protection with a moderate cap rate. Suitable for investors seeking a balance between protection and growth potential.
Protection: 0% Floor
Full capital protection — you cannot lose any of your investment regardless of index performance. If the index falls, your return is 0%. Upside is subject to the applicable cap rate, which is lower in return for complete downside protection. Suitable for risk-averse investors who prioritise preservation of capital.
Performance Scenarios
Each scenario represents a percentile outcome from 1,000 simulated paths, generated by resampling historical Euro Stoxx 50® annual returns. Scenarios illustrate the range of plausible outcomes; they are not forecasts.
Pessimistic — 10th Percentile
Represents adverse but plausible market conditions. 9 out of 10 simulated paths produced a better outcome. Intended to illustrate downside risk and the value of capital protection.
Average — 50th Percentile
The median outcome across all simulated paths. Half of simulations produced a higher result and half a lower result. This is not a guaranteed or expected return.
Optimistic — 90th Percentile
Represents favourable market conditions. Only 1 in 10 simulated paths produced a better outcome. Illustrates upside participation, subject to the applicable cap rate.
